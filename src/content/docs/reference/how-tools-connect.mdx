## Common paths through the site

### I want to understand the underlying trust model first

1. Read [in-toto attestations](/in-toto-attestations/).
2. Skim the relevant [ITE pages](/in-toto-ites/), especially
   [ITE-5](/in-toto-ites/ite-5/), [ITE-6](/in-toto-ites/ite-6/), and
   [ITE-7](/in-toto-ites/ite-7/).
3. Move to [Witness quick start](/witness/quick-start/) or
   [What is SSCS and why should I care?](/tutorials/what-is-sscs-and-why-should-i-care/).

### I need a practical build, sign, and verify loop

1. Start with [Local lab setup](/local-labs/setup-workspace/).
2. Run [Build, attest, sign, and verify](/local-labs/build-attest-sign-verify/).
3. Move to [GitHub Actions](/reference-pipelines/github-actions/) or
   [GitLab CI](/reference-pipelines/gitlab-ci/) when you are ready to carry the
   loop into CI.

### I care about AI agents and delegated policy

1. Start at [aflock](/aflock/).
2. Read [Agent identity](/aflock/agent-identity/) and
   [MCP and hooks](/aflock/mcp-integration/).
3. Continue with [Sublayouts](/aflock/sublayouts/) and
   [Session management](/aflock/session-management/).
4. Use [SPIFFE / SPIRE local development](/spiffe-spire/local-development/)
   when you want to ground identity with workload certificates.

### I need cluster-bound verification and admission decisions

1. Start with [Kubernetes-native SSCS](/kubernetes-sscs/).
2. Read [Attesting images and manifests](/kubernetes-sscs/attesting-images-and-manifests/).
3. Continue with [Verification and admission](/kubernetes-sscs/verification-and-admission/)
   and [Admission and policy enforcement](/kubernetes-sscs/admission-policy-enforcement/).
4. Cross-check [Sigstore Policy Controller](/sigstore/policy-controller/) and
   [Workload identity](/kubernetes-sscs/workload-identity/).

## When the boundaries matter

- Use [witness](/witness/) when you want CLI behavior you can run directly.
- Use [go-witness](/go-witness/) when you need to embed attestation or
  verification logic in your own Go code.
- Use [rookery](/rookery/) when the plugin inventory, packaging boundary, or
  hardened binary shape matters.
- Use [aflock](/aflock/) when the subject is AI-agent behavior and signed policy
  enforcement rather than a generic build pipeline.
- Use [Sigstore](/sigstore/) and [SPIFFE / SPIRE](/spiffe-spire/) when you are
  reasoning about who is allowed to sign, not just what got signed.
